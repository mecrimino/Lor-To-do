LOR TO-DO is a fast, beautiful, and completely offline to-do app. Install it, open it, and start adding tasks immediately. No sign-up, no cloud accounts, no ads, no telemetry, and strictly NO internet permission in the Android manifest.

Tagline: Tasks. Local. Yours.

Features:
• Zero Setup: Opens straight to your tasks without registration.
• 100% Offline: The INTERNET permission is never declared or used.
• Natural Language Quick-Add: Type "Pay rent tomorrow 6pm !high #bills every month" and watch it automatically set dates, priorities, tags, and recurrence offline.
• Smart Views: Today, Inbox, Upcoming 7 days, Overdue, Starred, and Completed.
• Modern Material 3 UI: Material You dynamic color matching, warm cheerful aesthetics, and true AMOLED black mode.
• Full Organization: Subtasks, tags, custom lists, priority markers, and drag reordering.
• Reliable Reminders: Exact alarms that survive phone reboots and Doze mode.
• Productivity Tools: Built-in Pomodoro focus timer, productivity statistics, and habit streaks.
• Kanban Board & Calendar Views: Visualize your workflow your way.
• Data Ownership: One-tap export and import in JSON, CSV, and Markdown.
• Open Source: Licensed under GNU GPL-3.0.
